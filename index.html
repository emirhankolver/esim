How it works

Step one

Pick a plan

Choose where you are going and how much data you need. One country, a whole region, or a global plan that follows you between continents. Prices are in credits, so the same plan costs the same wherever you buy it.

Step two

Install it before you fly

Add the eSIM to your phone in seconds over Wi-Fi. Your phone's own installer does the work — no shop, no plastic, no paperwork. The plan does not start counting until you land.

Step three

Land connected

Switch it on when you arrive. Data and days left are in the app, and on a home-screen widget if you would rather not open anything at all.

Good to know

Top up without reinstalling

Add data to the eSIM already on your phone. The profile stays where it is.

Changed your mind

Cancel a plan before you install it and the credits go straight back to your balance.

Your number is untouched

Your own SIM stays in the phone. Calls and messages keep working as they always did.

Get the app

Plans are data only. Milo does not provide a phone number, does not carry calls or SMS, and cannot reach emergency services. Questions? We have a help page.
